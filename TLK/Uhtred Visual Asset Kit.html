The graphical layer for Uhtred's speech: a 3×3 Instagram carousel prototype, an icon language for the stabilizer / destabilizer classes, wallpaper-type frames for each platform, and a Facebook post scaffold. Everything here maps back to the 22 cuts and 74 seconds in the cut sheet.

03 · Distabilitizement of Time — the 74-Second Axis

Segment width is proportional to real duration, so the eye reads pacing directly: the wide pale bands are where he lingers, the narrow ones where he clips through. Rust segments are the destabilizers — the two places the speech names a wound (lines 4, 12, 14).
